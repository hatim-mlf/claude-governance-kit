# Phase 2: intent and the buyer's journey

## The four intents

| Intent | The searcher wants | Typical words | Page that wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Informational** | to understand or learn | how to, what is, why, guide, tips | guide, explainer, tutorial |
| **Commercial** (investigation) | to compare options before choosing | best, vs, alternative, review, top, which, for beginners | comparison guide, "how to choose", criteria table |
| **Transactional** | to act now | download, free trial, buy, pricing, subscribe, app | product or feature page, pricing, store listing |
| **Navigational** | a specific brand or page | brand name, brand + login or support | home, login, support |

Comparing two products is **commercial**, not informational. It is the decision stage, where
the buyer chooses you or someone else, so it gets the most care.

**Verify intent on the live results page.** Search the keyword and read the top results. If
they are guides, the intent is informational, whatever the words suggest. If they are product
pages or store listings, it is transactional. Record what you saw, not what you assumed. A
mixed page of results means mixed intent: target the dominant one, and answer the other in a
section.

## The journey

| Stage | The buyer's state | Intent | Content job | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Awareness** | has a problem, may not know a product solves it | informational | answer the problem fully and honestly | soft: related guide, "see how X does it" |
| **Consideration** | knows solutions exist, is learning what matters | informational → commercial | teach the criteria, so they judge any option | feature page, comparison guide |
| **Decision** | shortlists and compares | commercial | criteria tables, objections answered, proof (screenshots, reviews, story) | try, download, start a trial |
| **Conversion** | ready to act | transactional | remove the last friction: price clarity, platform, trial terms | the one primary action |
| **Retention and advocacy** | a customer | navigational, informational | support, how-tos, shareable outputs | invite, review, share |

**A page serves one stage, and its CTA matches that stage.** A "buy now" on an awareness
guide converts badly and reads as an advert. A soft link on a decision page loses the sale.

## Mapping

For each priority persona, fill the grid **stage × cluster** from `keyword-map.csv`. Empty
cells are content gaps. Decision and conversion gaps cost revenue first, so fill them first,
even when their volume is lower than awareness keywords.

**Objections from phase 1 attach to the decision stage.** Each frequent objection becomes a
section or FAQ entry on the decision page that faces it.

## When the product has no on-site checkout

For a mobile app, the conversion step is the store listing, not a website page. Then:

- transactional pages on the site hand off to the store with a tracked link (campaign token);
- the store listing itself (title, subtitle, keyword field, screenshots, ratings) is the
  conversion page, and gets App Store Optimisation as part of this work;
- the site's transactional queries are mostly "<brand> app", "<feature> app iphone", and
  "download …".
